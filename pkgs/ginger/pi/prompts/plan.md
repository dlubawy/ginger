description: Create a plan to implement research ideas
---

Read the research files ./.agent/research/\*.md

Develop a plan to implement the ideas contained in the research.

Plan requirements:

- No open-ended implementation details
- Clear objectives with specific outcomes
- References to any research details if additional context is needed
- Include questions where additional details are needed

Write your plan to ./PLAN.md

Follow-up questions:

- Ask your questions, if there are any, after writing the plan
- Edit the ./PLAN.md based on any answers provided
- Repeat asking follow-ups until all questions are answered

Summarize the plan when you are done and all questions are answered
